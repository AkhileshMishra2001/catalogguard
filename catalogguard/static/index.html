01 / Identity-aware split

Positive match components, identical pixels and identical normalised titles stay in one partition. Cross-partition pairs are excluded.

02 / Frozen encoders

Multilingual MiniLM encodes titles. CLIP encodes photographs. Supervised classifiers combine similarity features. Backbones are not fine-tuned.

03 / Human approval

Suggested matches require review. Decisions are stored separately and never silently mixed into benchmark labels.

04 / Honest scope

Offline product-pair benchmark. No sales lift, exact-variant guarantee or automatic catalog merge is claimed.
